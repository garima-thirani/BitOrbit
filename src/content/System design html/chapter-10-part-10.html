📖 The Story — The Datacenter Fire
When Everything is Lost

An online banking platform runs inside one datacenter. One night, a fire destroys the entire facility. Servers gone. Storage gone. Backups stored inside the same datacenter also gone. The company loses every customer account.

The failure was not the fire. The failure was poor Disaster Recovery planning. High Availability handles routine failures. Disaster Recovery restores systems after catastrophic events. Both are essential for production systems.

🎯 What is Disaster Recovery?
Disaster Recovery (DR): The process of restoring systems after major failures including region failures, floods, fires, cyberattacks, or data corruption. DR planning defines RPO (maximum acceptable data loss), RTO (maximum acceptable downtime), and recovery procedures.

🔄 Failover & Failback
Failover: Automatically shifting traffic from failed primary to healthy secondary. Failback: Returning traffic to primary after recovery. Requires careful data synchronization to prevent data loss. Always test failover procedures regularly.
✅ Chaos Engineering
Chaos Engineering: Proactively testing system resilience by intentionally injecting failures. Netflix Chaos Monkey randomly terminates production servers. Goal: ensure applications recover automatically. Regular chaos experiments build confidence in DR procedures.
🏭 Production Examples
Real World

Netflix & GitHub

Netflix: Uses Chaos Monkey to randomly kill servers. Tests failover continuously. Simian Army includes Latency Monkey, Chaos Monkey, and Doctor Monkey. Applications must survive unexpected failures.

GitHub: Regularly performs disaster recovery exercises. Validates backup restoration and database failover. Ensures RTO and RPO targets are met.

🎯 Interview Callout
Q
"What's the difference between HA and DR?"
"High Availability minimizes service interruptions during component failures through redundancy and automatic failover. Disaster Recovery restores services after catastrophic failures such as regional outages, cyberattacks, or large-scale data corruption. HA handles server failures in seconds. DR handles region failures in minutes to hours."
🧠 Memory Trick
RPO = How much data can we lose? RTO = How long can we be down? Backup = Historical copy. Replication = Live copy. Chaos Engineering = Test failures.
