# Interview Questions & Solutions

## Question 1

### Question

What does CAP Theorem state?

### Ideal Answer

CAP Theorem states that when a network partition occurs in a distributed system, it is impossible to simultaneously guarantee both strong consistency and availability. Since network partitions are unavoidable, systems must choose between consistency and availability during such failures.

---

## Question 2

### Question

Why doesn't CA exist in distributed systems?

### Ideal Answer

Because distributed systems must tolerate network failures. During a partition, communication between nodes is interrupted, making partition tolerance mandatory. Therefore, the real trade-off is between consistency and availability.

---

## Question 3

### Question

Give examples of CP and AP systems.

### Ideal Answer

CP systems include banking systems, Google Spanner, and distributed lock services, where correctness is more important than availability. AP systems include Cassandra, social media feeds, analytics platforms, and recommendation engines, where continuous availability is preferred over immediate consistency.

---

## Question 4

### Question

When would you choose AP over CP?

### Ideal Answer

Choose AP when temporary inconsistency is acceptable and maintaining availability is more important. Examples include social media feeds, product reviews, recommendation systems, analytics dashboards, and logging platforms.

---

## Question 5 (FAANG)

### Question

Design the consistency strategy for an e-commerce platform.

### Ideal Answer

Use CP for payment processing, inventory reservation, authentication, and order confirmation because correctness is essential. Use AP for recommendations, product reviews, search indexing, analytics, notifications, and activity feeds where temporary inconsistency has little business impact. Different services should select consistency models based on business requirements rather than applying one policy across the entire system.

# Revision Notes

- CAP applies only during network partitions.
- Partition tolerance is mandatory in distributed systems.
- The real decision is CP vs AP.
- CP prioritizes correctness and may reject requests.
- AP prioritizes availability and may return stale data.
- Different services within the same application can choose different CAP trade-offs.
- CAP decisions should always be driven by business requirements.
