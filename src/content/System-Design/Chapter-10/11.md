# Interview Questions & Solutions

## Question 1

### Question

Why do planet-scale systems use CDNs?

### Ideal Answer

CDNs store static assets close to users, reducing latency, lowering origin server load, decreasing bandwidth costs, and improving availability by serving cached content from edge locations.

---

## Question 2

### Question

What is Edge Computing?

### Ideal Answer

Edge computing executes application logic near users instead of in centralized datacenters. This reduces latency, lowers backend load, and improves responsiveness for tasks such as authentication, request filtering, personalization, and image processing.

---

## Question 3

### Question

Why don't global systems store all data in one database?

### Ideal Answer

A single global database creates high latency, availability risks, scalability limits, and regulatory challenges. Planet-scale systems partition and replicate data across regions while keeping users close to their data.

---

## Question 4

### Question

What is Data Sovereignty?

### Ideal Answer

Data sovereignty requires that certain types of data remain within specific geographic or legal boundaries. System architects must design storage and replication strategies that comply with regional regulations such as GDPR and financial data localization laws.

---

## Question 5 (FAANG)

### Question

Design the infrastructure for a global social media platform serving two billion users.

### Ideal Answer

Deploy Active-Active regional clusters across major continents. Use global DNS with latency-based routing and health checks, CDNs for media delivery, edge computing for request preprocessing, regional caches, sharded databases with geo-replication, asynchronous replication for non-critical data, strong consistency for authentication and payments, comprehensive observability, autoscaling, and automated disaster recovery. Keep user data regionally localized where required while replicating metadata globally to support worldwide features.
