# Part 1 — Why Sharding Exists

## Problem

One database

eventually

reaches

hardware limits.

```text
More Users

↓

More Queries

↓

Higher CPU

↓

More Memory

↓

Disk Bottleneck

↓

Slow Database
```

---

## Vertical Scaling

Increase

resources

of

one server.

```text
16 GB RAM

↓

64 GB RAM

↓

256 GB RAM
```

### Advantages

✔ Simple

✔ Easy deployment

✔ Strong consistency

---

### Limitations

✘ Hardware limits

✘ Expensive

✘ Single point of failure

---

# Horizontal Scaling

Instead

of

one bigger server,

add

more servers.

```text
DB1

DB2

DB3

DB4
```

---

### Advantages

✔ Nearly unlimited growth

✔ Better fault tolerance

✔ Higher throughput

---

### Challenges

✘ Routing

✘ Coordination

✘ Distributed transactions

---

# What is Sharding?

Split

one logical database

into

multiple

physical databases.

---

Example

```text
1 Billion Users

↓

Shard 1

Shard 2

Shard 3

Shard 4
```

---

# Scale-Up vs Scale-Out

| Scale Up | Scale Out |
|------------|------------|
| Bigger server | More servers |
| Easier | More scalable |
| Hardware limit | Nearly unlimited |
| Simpler | More complex |

# Part 2 — Sharding Strategies

## Shard Key

Determines

where

data lives.

---

Good shard key

✔ High cardinality

✔ Stable

✔ Even distribution

✔ Frequently queried

---

Poor shard key

✘ Frequently updated

✘ Low cardinality

✘ Hotspots

---

# Range Sharding

Example

```text
1–250M

↓

Shard 1

------------

250M–500M

↓

Shard 2
```

### Advantages

✔ Range queries

✔ Easy routing

---

### Problems

✘ Hot partitions

---

# Hash Sharding

Formula

```text
Hash(Key)

MOD

Shards
```

---

### Advantages

✔ Even distribution

✔ Prevents hotspots

---

### Problems

✘ Poor range queries

---

# Directory Sharding

Lookup table

determines

target shard.

---

### Advantages

✔ Flexible

---

### Problems

✘ Extra metadata

---

# Geographic Sharding

Example

```text
US

↓

US Cluster

------------

Europe

↓

EU Cluster
```

---

### Benefits

✔ Low latency

✔ Data residency

---

# Tenant Sharding

One tenant

↓

One shard.

Ideal

for SaaS.

---

# User Sharding

One user

↓

One shard.

Ideal

for

social media.

---

# Composite Sharding

Combine

multiple strategies.

Example

```text
Region

+

Hash(UserID)
```

# Part 5 — Rebalancing

## Why?

Traffic

changes

over time.

Need

balanced shards.

---

# Hot Key

One record

receives

massive traffic.

---

Example

Celebrity account.

---

# Hot Partition

Entire shard

becomes overloaded.

---

# Rebalancing

Move

small portions

of data.

---

# Online Resharding

Move

data

without downtime.

---

Workflow

```text
Copy

↓

Sync

↓

Validate

↓

Cutover
```

---

---

# Split

# Merge

# Counter Sharding

later.

# Queue-Based Writes
