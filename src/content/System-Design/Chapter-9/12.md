# Production Anti-Patterns

Never

❌ Share databases

❌ Create synchronous dependency chains

❌ Couple deployments

❌ Ignore observability

❌ Skip retries and circuit breakers

❌ Build "God Services"

# Staff Engineer Takeaways

When evaluating a Microservices architecture, always ask:

1. What is the business capability?
2. Who owns the data?
3. Which communication should be synchronous?
4. Which should be asynchronous?
5. Where are the bottlenecks?
6. How does the system scale?
7. What happens when a service fails?
8. How is the system monitored?
9. How are deployments performed?
10. What are the trade-offs?
