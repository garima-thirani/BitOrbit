# The Golden Rules of Microservices

1. Design around business capabilities.
2. Every service owns its own data.
3. Never share databases.
4. Keep services independently deployable.
5. Prefer asynchronous communication where possible.
6. Every external call needs a timeout.
7. Retries require exponential backoff and jitter.
8. Every request needs a Trace ID.
9. Secure every service-to-service call.
10. Automate everything.

# Final Engineering Principles

1. Optimize for team autonomy, not just code organization.
2. Own your data—never share databases.
3. Keep synchronous paths short.
4. Make everything observable.
5. Automate deployments.
6. Design for failure from day one.
7. Secure every network hop.
8. Scale horizontally whenever possible.
9. Measure before optimizing.
10. Build systems that can evolve without stopping the business.
