# Step 5 — Explain Object Storage

Interviewers frequently ask:

> **Why not store movies in MySQL?**

Answer:

Movies are extremely large binary objects.

Object storage provides:

- Durability
- Scalability
- Lower cost
- Easy replication

Databases store metadata only.

---

# Step 6 — Explain CDN

Another common question:

> **Why use a CDN?**

Without a CDN:

```text
User

↓

Central Storage

↓

Movie
```

Every user hits the origin.

With a CDN:

```text
User

↓

Nearby Edge Server

↓

Movie
```

Latency decreases dramatically.

---

# Step 7 — Explain Open Connect

Netflix built its own CDN.

Advantages:

- Servers inside ISP networks
- Lower latency
- Lower bandwidth cost
- Better cache hit rate

This is one of Netflix's biggest competitive advantages.

---

# Step 8 — Explain Adaptive Bitrate Streaming

Interviewers often ask:

> **How does Netflix change quality without restarting playback?**

Answer:

Netflix stores multiple encoded versions.

```text
240p

480p

720p

1080p

4K
```

Each version is divided into identical segments.

The player simply requests the next segment

at another bitrate.

# Common Interview Questions

---

## Why Object Storage?

Because videos are:

- Large
- Immutable
- Cheap to store
- Easy to replicate

Databases are not optimized for large media.

---

## Why CDN?

Millions of users watch the same content.

CDNs reduce:

- Latency
- Origin load
- Bandwidth costs

---

## Why Open Connect?

Instead of relying entirely on cloud CDNs,

Netflix places caching appliances directly inside ISP networks.

Benefits:

- Faster playback
- Reduced internet congestion
- Lower operational costs

---

## Why Split Videos into Segments?

Benefits:

- Adaptive streaming
- Faster buffering
- Resume playback
- Efficient retries

Instead of downloading a 5 GB file,

clients download small chunks.

---

## Why Adaptive Bitrate Streaming?

Internet speed changes constantly.

ABR adjusts video quality automatically,

preventing playback interruptions.

---

## Why Kafka?

Playback events trigger:

- Recommendations
- Analytics
- Monitoring
- ML Training

Kafka enables asynchronous processing.

---

## How Does Continue Watching Work?

Only metadata is stored.

Example:

```text
Movie

↓

Timestamp

↓

Resume Position
```

The player later resumes from that position.

---

## How Are Recommendations Generated?

Multiple candidate lists are generated.

Machine Learning ranks them.

Examples include:

- Trending
- Similar Movies
- Watch History
- Collaborative Filtering

---

## What Happens if a CDN Fails?

The player switches to another edge server.

Streaming continues with minimal interruption.

---

## SQL Only

Advantages

- Simple

Disadvantages

---

## Cloud CDN Only

Advantages

- Easy to deploy

Disadvantages

- Higher bandwidth cost
- Less control

---

## Open Connect

Advantages

- Better performance
- Lower latency
- Lower bandwidth costs

Disadvantages

## Monolith

Advantages

- Easy development

Disadvantages

- Difficult scaling

---

## Microservices

Advantages

- Independent deployment
- Independent scaling

Disadvantages

- Operational complexity

# Common Interview Mistakes

---

## Mistake 1

Treating Netflix like YouTube.

Netflix primarily streams professionally produced content.

Uploads are limited.

---

## Mistake 2

Ignoring CDN.

Without CDNs,

global streaming becomes impossible.

---

## Mistake 3

Ignoring Adaptive Bitrate Streaming.

Changing network conditions are fundamental to streaming.

---

## Mistake 4

Storing videos inside SQL databases.

Large media belongs in object storage.

---

## Mistake 5

Ignoring Recommendations.

Netflix's business depends heavily on personalization.

---

## Mistake 6

Ignoring Watch Progress.

Continue Watching is a core user feature.
