# Capacity Estimation

## Concurrent Streams

Suppose:

```text
30 Million

Concurrent Streams
```

Average bitrate:

```text
5 Mbps
```

Bandwidth required:

```text
30 Million

×

5 Mbps

=

150 Tbps
```

This enormous bandwidth requirement explains why Netflix relies heavily on CDNs.

---

## Storage

Average movie:

```text
5 GB
```

Library:

```text
100,000 Titles

×

5 GB

=

500 TB
```

After multiple encodings (240p–4K),

actual storage reaches several petabytes.

# Why Netflix is Harder Than It Looks

A beginner imagines:

```text
User

↓

Server

↓

Video
```

A production Netflix system must also handle:

- Multiple video resolutions
- Adaptive bitrate streaming
- Global CDNs
- Resume playback
- Personalized recommendations
- Watch history
- DRM
- Video encoding pipelines
- Multi-region deployments
- Billions of streaming requests

Each requirement introduces another layer of distributed system complexity.

Imagine a global library.

Instead of keeping every book in one warehouse,

bringing video content closer to viewers.

- Streaming systems differ significantly from traditional web applications.
