# Interview Questions & Solutions

## Question 1

### Question

Why do companies like Netflix and LinkedIn prefer Kafka?

### Ideal Answer

Kafka provides extremely high throughput, horizontal scalability, durable event storage, replay capability, and support for multiple independent consumer groups, making it ideal for large-scale event-driven architectures.

---

## Question 2

### Question

Why is RabbitMQ commonly used for workflow systems?

### Ideal Answer

RabbitMQ offers flexible routing through exchanges, low-latency message delivery, acknowledgments, retries, and mature queue semantics, making it well suited for background jobs, notifications, and workflow orchestration.

---

## Question 3

### Question

Why are idempotency keys critical in payment systems?

### Ideal Answer

Distributed systems may retry messages after failures. Idempotency keys ensure duplicate payment requests produce only one successful transaction, preventing customers from being charged multiple times.

---

## Question 4

### Question

Why do large companies build event-driven architectures?

### Ideal Answer

Event-driven architectures decouple services, improve scalability, isolate failures, simplify adding new consumers, and enable multiple independent systems to react to the same business event.

---

## Question 5 (FAANG)

### Question

Design the messaging platform for a global e-commerce company similar to Amazon.

### Ideal Answer

Use Kafka for business events such as orders, payments, inventory, and customer activity. Partition topics by entity ID to preserve ordering where necessary. Use consumer groups for independent services including shipping, analytics, fraud detection, recommendations, and notifications. Employ RabbitMQ for workflow-oriented tasks requiring advanced routing. Implement retries with exponential backoff, dead-letter queues, idempotent consumers, monitoring, and horizontal scaling to achieve high throughput and fault tolerance.
