# Engineering Notes

# Decision Framework

| Requirement | Recommended Approach |
|--------------|----------------------|

# Interview Questions & Solutions

## Question 1

### Question

Why is At-Least-Once delivery more common than Exactly-Once delivery?

### Ideal Answer

At-Least-Once delivery is significantly simpler to implement while ensuring that messages are not lost. Duplicate processing is handled through idempotent consumers, whereas Exactly-Once delivery requires complex coordination between producers, brokers, consumers, and storage systems.

---

## Question 2

### Question

What is idempotency, and why is it important?

### Ideal Answer

Idempotency means performing the same operation multiple times produces the same final result. It is essential because distributed messaging systems may redeliver messages after failures, and consumers must safely ignore duplicate processing.

---

## Question 3

### Question

Why is exponential backoff preferred over immediate retries?

### Ideal Answer

Immediate retries can overload an already failing service. Exponential backoff gradually increases retry delays, giving dependent systems time to recover while reducing retry storms.

---

## Question 4

### Question

What is a Dead Letter Queue?

### Ideal Answer

A Dead Letter Queue stores messages that repeatedly fail processing after exceeding a configured retry limit. This prevents problematic messages from blocking normal processing and allows engineers to inspect failures separately.

---

## Question 5 (FAANG)

### Question

Design a reliable payment processing system using Kafka.

### Ideal Answer

Use Kafka with replicated partitions and At-Least-Once delivery. Each payment message carries a globally unique idempotency key stored in the payment database. Consumers commit offsets only after successful processing. Failed operations retry using exponential backoff with jitter, and messages exceeding retry limits move to a Dead Letter Queue. Monitor consumer lag, retry counts, offset commits, and DLQ growth to ensure reliable processing under failures.

# Revision Notes

- Delivery guarantees define the balance between performance and reliability.
- At-Most-Once prioritizes speed, while At-Least-Once prioritizes reliability.
- Exactly-Once processing is complex and usually requires additional coordination.
- Consumer groups rebalance automatically when consumers join or leave.
- Exponential backoff with jitter prevents retry storms.
- Idempotency allows duplicate messages to be processed safely.
- Dead Letter Queues isolate permanently failing messages for later investigation.
