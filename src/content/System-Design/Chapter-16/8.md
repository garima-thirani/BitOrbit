# Part 1 — Why Message Queues Exist

## Traditional Communication

```text
Client

↓

Service A

↓

Service B

↓

Service C
```

Every service

waits

for

the next one.

---

Problems

❌ High latency

❌ Tight coupling

❌ Cascading failures

❌ Poor scalability

---

## Queue-Based Communication

```text
Client

↓

Service

↓

Queue

↓

Worker
```

The client

receives

an immediate response.

---

Background workers

process

the task later.

---

Benefits

✔ Lower latency

✔ Loose coupling

✔ Better scalability

✔ Higher availability

---

# Part 2 — Queue vs Pub/Sub

## Queue

```text

↓

Queue

↓

One Consumer
```

---

## Publish-Subscribe

```text
Publisher

↓

Topic

↓

Subscriber A

Subscriber B

Subscriber C
```

Each subscriber

---

# Comparison

| Queue | Pub/Sub |
|---------|----------|
| One Consumer | Many Consumers |
| Task Processing | Event Distribution |
| Work Queue | Broadcast |
| Competing Consumers | Independent Subscribers |
