# What is Event Storming?

Event Storming is a collaborative workshop used to discover how a business works by identifying the events that occur within it.

Instead of asking

> "What tables do we need?"

We ask

> "What actually happens?"

Business comes first.

Software comes later.

# Interview Questions & Solutions

## Question 1

### Question

Why perform Event Storming before designing microservices?

### What Interviewer Tests

Whether you understand domain-first architecture.

### Ideal Answer

Event Storming identifies business workflows, domain boundaries, and ownership before implementation. It helps teams discover natural service boundaries and prevents designing microservices around technical layers rather than business capabilities.

---

## Question 2

### Question

What is the difference between a Command and an Event?

### Ideal Answer

A Command expresses intent and asks the system to perform an action. An Event records that something has already happened. Commands may fail; events represent successful business facts.

---

## Question 3

### Question

What is an Aggregate?

### Ideal Answer

An Aggregate is a consistency boundary that owns business rules and state. Commands are validated by the Aggregate, which produces domain events if business rules are satisfied.

---

## Question 4

### Question

What is a Process Manager?

### Ideal Answer

A Process Manager coordinates long-running workflows involving multiple services. It reacts to events, issues commands, tracks progress, and handles workflow decisions without embedding business logic into individual services.

---

## Question 5 (FAANG)

### Question

How would you discover service boundaries for an e-commerce platform?

### Ideal Answer

Conduct an Event Storming workshop with engineers and domain experts. Identify business events, derive commands, discover aggregates, group related events into bounded contexts, and use those contexts to define microservice boundaries. This produces services aligned with business capabilities rather than technical implementation.
